## Prayer Focus

Today we lift up communities in regions affected by conflict and natural disaster. We pray for:

- **Peace** — That violence would cease and dialogue would prevail.
- **Provision** — That food, water, and medical supplies would reach those in need.
- **Protection** — Especially for children and vulnerable families.

## Scripture

> "Blessed are the peacemakers, for they will be called children of God." — Matthew 5:9
