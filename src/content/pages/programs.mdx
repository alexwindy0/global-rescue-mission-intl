## Sustainable initiatives for lasting change

Our programs are designed to serve people holistically and with lasting impact.

### Midnight Prayer Network

A global community of believers praying for mission fields and vulnerable communities.

### Child Sponsorship Program

Monthly support for children’s education, nutrition, and healthcare.

### Volunteer Deployment

Training and deployment opportunities for short-term and long-term mission service.

### Disaster Response Team

Rapid-response teams prepared to serve immediately after crisis events.
