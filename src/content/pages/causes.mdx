## Focused efforts where the need is greatest

We support causes that create immediate relief and long-term transformation in vulnerable communities.

### Emergency Relief

Rapid response to disasters with food, water, shelter, and medical support.

### Education

School building, teacher training, and scholarship support to unlock opportunity.

### Healthcare

Mobile clinics and medical resources for underserved communities.

### Clean Water

Safe drinking water access through wells and filtration systems.

### Food Security

Sustainable agriculture and emergency food assistance for families in need.

### Evangelism

Sharing the Gospel and caring for whole communities through holistic outreach.
