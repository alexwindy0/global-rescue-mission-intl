## Your skills can change lives

Volunteers are essential to this mission. Whether you serve locally or overseas, your time and talents can directly strengthen communities.

### Why volunteer?

- Direct impact in communities that need support most
- Training and support for mission service
- Short-term and long-term opportunities available
- A global community of like-minded servants
- Support for approved deployment needs
