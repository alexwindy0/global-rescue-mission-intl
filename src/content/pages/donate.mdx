## Ways to Give

Your generosity enables us to respond quickly to crises and build sustainable change in vulnerable communities.

### Support emergency response

Provide food, medicine, shelter, and recovery support.

### Support education

Fund school construction, teacher training, and scholarships.

### Support healthcare

Help fund clinics and medical supplies for underserved communities.

All donations are tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law.
